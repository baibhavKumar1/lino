Privacy Policy

Application: Lino
Effective Date: October 13, 2026
Developer: Wishop Technologies

Welcome to Lino ("we," "our," or "us"). We provide an intelligent grocery management, pantry inventory, and AI-powered meal ideation platform. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

Core Commitment: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data, shopping history, or dietary preferences to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

1 Information We Collect

When you interact with Lino, we may collect the following types of information to provide and improve our service:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile photo when signing in through Google Sign-In or registering via email authentication.
  • Pantry and Grocery Data: Inventory items, custom grocery entries, quantities, category selections, and shopping cart lists.
  • Culinary Preferences: Household size, appliances, dietary choices (e.g., Veg, Non-Veg, Jain), food allergies, and meal schedule entries.
  • AI Chef Conversations: In-app chat messages, meal queries, and recipe generation requests sent to our culinary copilot.
  • Camera and Image Data: Photos taken through the Receipt Scanner feature, processed solely to extract item names and quantities. Images are not retained or utilized for facial identification.
  • Device Diagnostics: Basic non-identifying telemetry including operating system version and crash reports to maintain application stability.

2 How We Use Your Information

Your data is used strictly for core app operations and features:

  • To personalize culinary suggestions and recipes based on your in-stock pantry items
  • To synchronize your inventory, shopping lists, and meal schedules across your devices
  • To process grocery receipts and automatically populate your kitchen pantry
  • To maintain secure authentication and safeguard your account
  • To identify technical bugs and enhance overall app responsiveness

3 Third-Party Services and Data Processors

We work with trusted third-party providers who process data strictly under our direction and industry-standard security measures:

  • Google Sign-In / Google Play Services: Used for secure identity verification and authentication management.
  • Neon PostgreSQL: Cloud database infrastructure with TLS/SSL encryption for secure storage of your inventory and session records.
  • OpenRouter API: Natural language AI processing used to generate contextual cooking recipes and ingredient recommendations. Prompt data does not include personally identifiable records.

4 Data Security and Storage

We implement rigorous administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your information. All communications between the Lino mobile application and our backend database are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

5 Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your kitchen and account data for as long as your account remains active. You maintain full ownership and control over your data.

How to Request Complete Data Deletion:

You can request permanent deletion of your account and all associated records (pantry items, chat logs, saved recipes, and profile information) at any time by emailing us at zyx.pohsiw@ramuk.vahbiab with the subject line "Data Deletion Request". Requests are executed within 7 business days.

6 Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Lino is not designed for or directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete it from our servers.

7 Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

Depending on your location, you may possess the following privacy rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification: Update or correct inaccuracies in your saved profile.
  • Right to Erasure: Request the deletion of your account and all associated culinary data.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request restrictions on how your data is processed.

8 Updates to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our service or legal standards. We will notify you of any material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
